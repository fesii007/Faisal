FAQ

Questions clients
always ask.

What do you need to start? +
A clear description of what you want to build, who it's for, what it needs to do, and your timeline. The more detail, the more accurate the estimate.
How long does a project take? +
A landing page: 3–5 days. A full web app: 2–4 weeks depending on scope. I give a timeline estimate before starting and stick to it.
Do you provide the source code? +
Yes — always. You receive full ownership of all code, assets, and database. Nothing is locked behind my account.
Can you work with my existing codebase? +
Yes. I regularly work on existing projects — adding features, fixing bugs, refactoring, or rebuilding specific parts. Share the repo and I'll review it first.
Do you handle deployment? +
Yes. I deploy to Vercel, Netlify, cPanel, AWS, or wherever you prefer. I configure the environment and make sure it runs properly before handoff.
What about revisions? +
Unlimited revisions until you're satisfied. I don't count rounds or charge extra. We work until the deliverable is exactly right.
Do you work with agencies? +
Yes. White-label development for agencies is a significant part of my work. I deliver clean, commented, client-ready code with no branding.
What payment methods do you accept? +
Fiverr, Upwork, PayPal, and bank transfer. For direct projects I require a 50% deposit to begin, remainder on completion.
